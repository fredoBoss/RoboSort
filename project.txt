Project Name: RoboSort

Overview:
RoboSort is a mobile robot rover designed to autonomously pick up trash and sort it into two categories: biodegradable (bio) and non-biodegradable (non-bio). The robot navigates its environment, detects and avoids obstacles, collects trash using a mechanical arm, and deposits it into the correct bin via a conveyor and sorting mechanism.

---

Hardware Architecture:

1. Main Boards:
   - Orange Pi 1: Primary computing board responsible for computer vision (trash detection and classification into bio/non-bio).
   - Arduino Mega: Secondary controller handling all low-level drive and actuator control.

2. Movement System:
   - 4x DC Motors: Drive the rover's wheels for omnidirectional or differential movement.
   - 4x Ultrasonic Sensors: Mounted around the rover for real-time obstacle detection and avoidance.

3. Trash Collection Mechanism:
   - Servo Motors (Lift): Raises and lowers the trash-picking arm.
   - Servo Motors (Stretch/Scope): Extends and retracts the arm to reach trash items.
   - 1x DC Motor (Conveyor): Drives a conveyor belt that transports collected trash from the arm to the sorting bin area.

4. Sorting Mechanism:
   - 1x Servo Motor (Sorting Arm/Trigger): Actuates a gate or arm that directs trash into either the bio or non-bio bin based on classification received from the Orange Pi.

---

System Communication:
- The Orange Pi 1 runs the computer vision pipeline to classify detected trash.
- Classification results are sent to the Arduino Mega (e.g., via UART/Serial).
- The Arduino Mega executes all physical actions: motor driving, obstacle avoidance logic, conveyor control, arm movement, and sorting trigger.

---

Software Components:
- Computer Vision (Orange Pi 1): Image capture and inference to classify trash as biodegradable or non-biodegradable.
- Drive Control (Arduino Mega): Motor speed/direction control, ultrasonic-based obstacle avoidance, servo and conveyor sequencing, and sorting actuation.

---

Workflow:
1. Rover navigates autonomously while ultrasonic sensors detect and avoid obstacles.
2. Camera on the Orange Pi detects a trash item in the rover's path.
3. The arm servos extend and lower to pick up the trash.
4. The conveyor motor transports the trash toward the bin area.
5. The Orange Pi classifies the trash (bio or non-bio) and signals the Arduino.
6. The sorting servo triggers to direct the trash into the correct bin.
7. The rover resumes navigation and repeats the cycle.
